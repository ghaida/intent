# v1.7.0 — /present

- New skill: `/present` turns finished design work into a deck.
  - It interviews you to understand your presentation goals, audience, and outputs.
  - It gets the story outline approved before building.
  - It builds in Google Slides, PowerPoint, Figma Slides or reveal.js.
  - It refuses invented numbers and marks gaps instead of filling them.
- Noor now owns `/present`. Decks moved off `/specify` and Rune.
- `/intent` now routes to `/storytelling`.
